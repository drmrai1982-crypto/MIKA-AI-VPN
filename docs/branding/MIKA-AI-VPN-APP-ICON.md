# MIKA AI VPN — Approved App Icon Design

Status: **Design approved**

The approved visual direction is the 3D neon cyber-security emblem shown in the final design preview.

## Required visual specification

- Main emblem: detailed split-color digital brain, electric blue on the left and neon green on the right.
- Security frame: metallic gold shield with a prominent gold padlock.
- Energy details: luminous cyan/green orbital ring and fine circuit-board traces on a deep black background.
- Brand name: **Mika AI** in large, clearly legible white/silver lettering. It must not be reduced to a small caption.
- Product label: **VPN** below the brand name, balanced in size with the brand name (not disproportionately oversized).
  - **V** — neon red
  - **P** — neon blue
  - **N** — neon green
- Overall style: premium, high-contrast, dimensional 3D cyberpunk/neon aesthetic.

## Integration notes

- Keep the artwork centered and within Android adaptive-icon safe areas.
- Prepare adaptive foreground/background resources and launcher density assets from the approved artwork.
- Verify the icon in round and square launcher masks and at small sizes.
- Do not change VPN engine, protocol, tunnel, or connection logic as part of this branding update.

The approved artwork itself still needs to be added as a binary image asset and converted into Android launcher resources. This document records the approved design requirements; it does not claim that the final artwork has already been installed in the app.
